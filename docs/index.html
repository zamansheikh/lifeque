নামাজ, শেখা আর প্রতিদিনের হিসাব — এক জায়গায় Prayer times and daily life, now fully in Bangla

An Android app for prayer times, the salah guide, medicines, expenses and tasks. Every screen reads in Bangla or English, and nothing leaves your phone.

What's new in 2.0

  • The whole app in Bangla — text, numerals and a 12-hour clock that reads the way people say it (সন্ধ্যা ৬:১২, not 6:12 PM).
  • Bangla by default, with the language picker on the very first screen.
  • Learn — the salah guide, surahs and du'as fully translated, with 12 surahs added from Al-Asr to An-Nas.
  • Raf' al-Yadain — a new section giving each school's position and the narrations behind it.
  • Medicines per person — keep a course for yourself, a parent or a child, add one in fewer taps, and get asked about a dose that slipped by instead of it being quietly written off.
  • Expenses — the category is guessed from the item name, in Bangla or English.
  • Home-screen widgets in Bangla, in the app's own typeface.

What's inside

🕌

Prayer times · নামাজের সময়

Accurate times for your location, jamaat times, alarms per waqt, a Qibla compass, and the makruh windows marked so you know when not to pray.

📖

Learn · শিখুন

A step-by-step salah guide for every prayer — including witr, janazah, eid and tarawih — plus the short surahs, du'as and adhkar, with the Arabic kept as written.

📿

Tasbih & calendar · তাসবিহ ও ক্যালেন্ডার

A counter for your dhikr, and a month view carrying the Gregorian, Hijri and Bangla dates together — shareable as an image.

💊

Medicines · ওষুধ

Courses kept per person, dose reminders that follow up when one is missed, and a history you can trust because nothing is marked for you.

🧾

Expenses · খরচ

Shopping lists with prices, monthly and per-category budgets, and categories guessed from what you typed — in either language.

✅

Tasks & study · কাজ ও পড়াশোনা

Tasks with priorities and recurrence, reminders, birthdays, a to-do list, and a Pomodoro timer for study sessions.

Your data stays yours

LifeQue has no account and no server. Prayer settings, medicines, expenses and tasks are stored on your phone, and your location is used to calculate prayer times without ever being sent anywhere. Read the privacy policy.
